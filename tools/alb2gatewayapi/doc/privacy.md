# Privacy

ALB exports can contain customer names, domains, IPs, namespaces, Service names,
and certificate references. Treat `input/` and `output/` as private.

Do not publish or upload:

- raw exports or generated reports;
- kubeconfigs, tokens, passwords, or Secret data;
- certificate private keys;
- customer/environment names or real addresses in examples or issues.

Examples in this directory use reserved example domains and fictional names.
When asking for help, redact secrets and unrelated customer data while keeping
namespace/name/label relationships consistent enough to reproduce the issue.
